How testing works here

  1. Backtest (this page): see how settings would have traded over the venue's daily history, with fees. It's quick, and you can change anything.
  2. Paper strategy: when a result looks worth watching, start it on paper with one click. It trades live Kraken prices with simulated money.
  3. Evidence: G1 needs the research loop's out-of-sample tests. G2 needs at least six weeks of paper results inside the backtest's range.
  4. Live: only after you approve G2, and only on a Kraken account whose key you install on the server.

Pick an instrument and a model on the left and press Run backtest. The address of the result page can be bookmarked or shared.
